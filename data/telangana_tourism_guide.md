## Telangana overview
Telangana is located on the Deccan Plateau in southern India. Its tourism attractions include historical monuments, forts, temples, lakes, forests, waterfalls, cultural festivals, food and nature destinations. Hyderabad is the state capital and combines historic landmarks with modern technology and business districts.

## Hyderabad
Hyderabad is known for its history, cultural heritage, food and modern development. The city was founded in 1591 by Muhammad Quli Qutb Shah. Major landmarks and attractions include Charminar, Golconda Fort, Qutb Shahi Tombs, Falaknuma Palace, Chowmahalla Palace, Salar Jung Museum and the Statue of Equality. Hyderabad is also known for Hyderabadi biryani, Irani chai and traditional bazaars such as Laad Bazaar and Sultan Bazaar. HITEC City is part of the city's modern technology hub.

## Charminar
Charminar is an iconic historic landmark in Hyderabad. Telangana Tourism describes it as having been built in 1591 by Muhammad Quli Qutb Shah. It is an important symbol of the city's heritage and is located in the historic part of Hyderabad.

## Golconda Fort
Golconda Fort is a major heritage attraction in Hyderabad associated with the Qutb Shahi rulers. It is known for its historic fort structures and architectural heritage.

## Warangal
Warangal is a historic city associated with the Kakatiya rulers and is recognized for its heritage architecture. Telangana Tourism describes it as approximately 150 km from Hyderabad. Attractions include Warangal Fort, the Thousand Pillar Temple and nearby heritage and nature destinations.

## Warangal Fort
Warangal Fort is an important landmark associated with Kakatiya architecture. It is known for its gateways, stone carvings and defensive structures. The Kakatiya Gateway is a notable feature.

## Thousand Pillar Temple
The Thousand Pillar Temple is located at the base of Hanamkonda hill in Warangal. It is dedicated to Shiva, Vishnu and Surya and is noted for carved pillars, rock-cut elephants and a carved Nandi.

## Ramappa Temple
Ramappa Temple, also known as the Ramalingeswara Temple, is a notable heritage site in Telangana and is recognized as a UNESCO World Heritage Site. It is associated with Kakatiya-era architecture.

## Nature and wildlife
Telangana's attractions include lakes, forests, hills and wildlife areas. Examples mentioned by Incredible India include Kinnerasani Wildlife Sanctuary, Pakhal Wildlife Sanctuary, Laknavaram Lake and Ananthagiri Hills. Check official sources for current access conditions and visitor information.

## Travel planning guidance
A visitor interested in heritage can explore Hyderabad landmarks such as Charminar, Golconda Fort and Chowmahalla Palace. A visitor interested in Kakatiya history can consider Warangal Fort, Thousand Pillar Temple and Ramappa Temple. Nature-oriented visitors can research destinations such as Laknavaram Lake, Ananthagiri Hills and wildlife sanctuaries.
